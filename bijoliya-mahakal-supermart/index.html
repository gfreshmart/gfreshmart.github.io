About This Store

A G Fresh Mart Store in Bijoliya, Bhilwara District

Mahakal Supermart is a G Fresh Mart franchise store in Bijoliya, Rajasthan, run by franchise owner Piyush. The store covers 1,100 sq ft and was set up with a total investment of approximately ₹25 lakh, built to the brand's green store theme.

The store's own category board lists groceries, grains, dairy, supplies, kitchenware, frozen food, toiletries and kids' snacks. In practice that means a full daily-needs shop: atta, rice and sugar, loose dals, cooking oils, spices and masalas, tea, snacks, home care and personal care, with kitchenware and general merchandise along the front.

How big is this store?+

Mahakal Supermart is 1,100 sq ft. A store this size works as a daily-needs format — groceries, packaged foods, personal care and household essentials — and needs clear road frontage and space for stock deliveries.

What did it cost to open?+

The owner reports a total investment of approximately ₹25 lakh for this 1,100 sq ft store in Bijoliya. Costs at another location differ with city, store size, rent and the condition of the space, so treat this as one real example rather than a quotation.

What does the store stock?+

Groceries, grains, dairy, kitchenware, frozen food, toiletries and kids' snacks — a full daily-needs shop, from atta, rice and loose dals through to spices, tea, snacks, home care, personal care and general merchandise.
