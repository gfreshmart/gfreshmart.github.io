About This Store

A Daily-Needs Supermarket Built for a 2 km Catchment

G Fresh Mart Vijay Nagar sits on the ground floor of a residential complex on Scheme 54 Road, about 400 metres from the main market. The store opened in March 2024 in a 2,400 sq ft unit and stocks around 11,000 products — daily groceries, fresh fruit and vegetables, dairy, packaged foods, personal care, and a small home-and-kitchen section.

Most customers live within two kilometres and shop two or three times a week, so the store is laid out for fast daily-needs shopping: 1.4 metre aisles, two billing counters, and a fresh section refilled every morning. Nine people work here, including the owner, who is on the floor most mornings.

The Franchise Owner

Meera Rathi ran a textile distribution business in Indore for eleven years before looking for something less seasonal. They wanted a business that could be run hands-on from a single location, with a supply chain they would not have to build themselves.

Retail was new ground. The first three months went into learning stock rotation on fresh produce, which the G Fresh Mart regional team reviewed with the store weekly until wastage settled.

The Local Market

Vijay Nagar has added several residential towers since 2019, but the nearest organised supermarket was more than three kilometres away. Most households were splitting their shopping between a kirana store and a monthly drive to a big-format store.

The Scheme 54 Road site was picked for exactly that gap: ground floor, 2,400 sq ft, roughly 4,000 households within two kilometres, and parking — which matters in an evening-peak catchment.

What This Store Received From G Fresh Mart

Site Selection

Two locations surveyed with the regional team, including footfall counts at both, before the final site was approved.

Store Design & Fit-Out

Layout, racking plan and signage drawings supplied by G Fresh Mart and executed by a local contractor.

Supply Chain

Onboarded to the regional distribution centre before opening, with a weekly replenishment cycle from week one.

Staff Training

Nine team members trained over ten days on billing, stock handling, fresh-section care and customer service.

Launch Marketing

Local leaflet distribution, opening offers and a WhatsApp catalogue set up for the first month of trading.

Ongoing Operations

A monthly visit from the regional manager, with category, pricing and planogram updates shared centrally.

Frequently Asked Questions

About this store and what it would take to open one like it.

How big is this store, and what does a store this size need?+

2,400 sq ft of carpet area on the ground floor with 12 parking spaces. A neighbourhood-format G Fresh Mart usually works in 1,500–3,000 sq ft with clear road frontage, a single level, and a shutter wide enough for stock deliveries.

How long did this store take to open?+

65 days from the first franchise enquiry in December 2023 to opening on 14 March 2024. That covered site shortlisting, agreement, fit-out, staff training and stocking. Timelines vary mainly with how quickly a suitable site is finalised.

What did the ₹38–42 lakh investment include?+

Franchise fee, interiors and fit-out, racking and equipment, opening inventory, and branding and launch marketing. Rent, security deposit and working capital were separate and depend entirely on the site.

Did the owner have retail experience before this?+

No. Meera Rathi ran a textile distribution business for eleven years. Store layout, supplier onboarding and staff training were handled with the G Fresh Mart regional team, and fresh-produce stock rotation was reviewed weekly for the first three months.

Can I visit this store before I apply?+

Yes. Store visits are arranged through the G Fresh Mart franchise team, subject to the owner's availability. The store is open 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM, all seven days.

Is a similar store possible in my city?+

Tell us your city and the space you have or are considering. The franchise team will share which formats are workable there, the space and frontage needed, and an indicative investment range for that location.
